### Issues

#### Critical

- 10A184 - The old ghost identifies himself as one of the fools punished for crossing life and death’s boundary - “He tried…” changes his confession into a statement about someone else, obscuring his connection to the ritual and subsequent “we” explanations - Suggested EN: “I am one of the fools who tried to cross the border between life and death... and paid the price, my boy.”

#### Important

- 10A45C - George realizes Alighieri was “seized by death,” fulfilling the books’ warning - “Death got you after all” sounds like ordinary death and weakens the explicit callback to 10B524 and the warning immediately above - Suggested EN: “...So you were seized by death after all...”

- 10A688 - Shinfa apologizes for borrowing Alicia’s body and offers to restrain the messenger - “I'm sorry” and “I'll hold” drift from her prescribed soft, formal voice, particularly noticeable after “No, there is no need for that” - Suggested EN: “I am sorry for borrowing Alicia-san's body...” / “I will hold the messenger of death back for a while!”

- 10ABD0 - Shinfa thanks George and returns Alicia before departing - “It's all thanks to you” and “I'll... give Alicia-san back” repeat the contraction drift during her farewell - Suggested EN: “It is all thanks to you.” / “I will... return Alicia-san to you...”

#### Minor

- 10ABD0 - Alicia dismisses the strange events as insufficient evidence of ghosts - “Asking me to believe in ghosts over that much” is awkward English and blunts her final skeptical punchline - Suggested EN: “Sure, a lot of weird stuff happened, but... asking me to believe in ghosts just because of that... is an insult to human intelligence!”

### Assessment

issue - The old ghost’s self-identification becomes third-person exposition, the “seized by death” callback weakens, and Shinfa’s voice drifts from the agreed formal register.
