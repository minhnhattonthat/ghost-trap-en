### Issues

#### Critical

- **11DB88** - 「ちょっと、カロルさん、どーしちゃったわけ？」: Alicia asks Karol what has happened to him - “Hey, Karol-san, what's gotten into him?” incorrectly switches to a third person while addressing Karol - **Suggested EN:** “...Hey, Karol-san, what's gotten into you?”

#### Important

- **1090C8** - 「さっきジョージさんをたすけた時に使った、私のタリスマンが、ききませんでしたから」: the talisman used to rescue George did not work against Alicia’s abductor - “When I saved you earlier ... the talisman I used didn't work on it” attaches the failure to George’s rescue, confusing two encounters - **Suggested EN:** “The Talisman I used to save you earlier, George-san... / it didn't work on the ghost that took Alicia-san.”

- **1096B4** - 「私がアリギエリさんを止めていれば」: Karol regrets not stopping Alighieri-san - “Mr. Alighieri” drops the deliberately retained honorific and breaks Karol’s usage elsewhere - **Suggested EN:** “If only I had stopped Alighieri-san...”

- **10ABD0** - 「思うだけならあんたのかって」: Karol is free merely to *think* life is worthless and should end - “If you think life's worthless and want to end it, hey, that's your business” omits the limiting “merely thinking,” weakening George’s rejection of acting on that belief - **Suggested EN:** “You can think life's worthless and should end all you like... / but whether I live or die is MY call!”

- **11DB88** - 「クリスタルはクリティカルがスゴイ」: Crystal has impressive critical hits, with wordplay leading into George’s complaint - “Crystals are crystal clear on Criticals” is difficult to interpret and loses the useful item hint - **Suggested EN:** “Crystal's critical hits really rock!” George’s “A pun!?” can then follow naturally.

#### Minor

- **106B04** - 「氏の仲間もいなくなって数年」: several years after Alighieri’s companions disappeared - “after his friends had gone too,” immediately following his death, can imply they were also known to have died - **Suggested EN:** “and a few years after his friends had disappeared,”

- **107D64** - 「次なる道をのぞむならば書とともにすすむがよい」: carry the book to reach the next path - “If thou seekest” introduces archaic English contrary to the glossary’s plain-language ruling - **Suggested EN:** “If you seek the next path, go forth with the book.”

- **109ECC** - 「私たちはアリギエリ氏を一度死なせ」: they caused Alighieri’s death before reviving him - “We made Mr. Alighieri die once” is conspicuously literal and awkward for Shinfa’s composed explanation - **Suggested EN:** “First, we brought about Mr. Alighieri's death, / then performed a ritual to bring him back.”

- **103FE8** - 「スタートボタンのミニマップでかくにんできる」: START opens access to the map of visited locations - “Places ... show up on the mini map with the START Button” attaches the button phrase awkwardly - **Suggested EN:** “I can press the START Button / to check places I've visited on the mini map.”

- **104890** - 「スタートボタンのUSEコマンド、アイテムをえらび、Aボタン」: open the menu, select USE and an item, then press A - “The USE command on the START Button” makes the command sound physically located on the button - **Suggested EN:** “Press the START Button, choose USE, / pick the item, then press the A Button. Right.”

- **126BF0** - 「きえろ！！！」: George forcefully orders the messenger of death to disappear - “Begone!!!” sounds like the formal ghosts rather than George’s established modern voice - **Suggested EN:** “Disappear!!! You messenger of death!!”
