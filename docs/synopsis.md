Cutscene strings (scene table, `tools/debug/scripts.tsv` scripts 0-38) in story order.

- **Prologue (106B04, script 0):** the Alighieri mansion, older than any record. ~20 years ago the world-famous
  artist Alighieri (paintings of life and death) bought it as a country home/studio and lived there with his
  "friends". After he died of illness and the friends vanished, rumours spread (glowing figures, moaning, people
  trapped). A research team surveyed it; 6 of 9 went missing; a survivor screamed that ghosts attacked them. The
  house became known as "Ghost Trap".
- **Opening (1072F8, script 2; 106DB4 = the same scenes from "The man who'd called..."):** George, a self-styled pro
  ghost hunter, narrates. Three days earlier his childhood friend Alicia called ghost hunting a scam; to prove her
  wrong he dragged her to the mansion. A man waved from a 2nd-floor window; they ran in; the door slammed shut. The
  man, Karol, had come to gawk at the ghost house and had been warning them NOT to come in. Doors and windows won't
  break. Karol's leg was yanked by something invisible; he can't walk. George explores alone; Alicia stays with
  Karol. Talking to them with the A Button gives advice.
- **107AA4 (script 3):** George returns to the hall; no exit found. Alicia is sceptical but worried ("be careful").
- **Books (109BF4.. 109E24):** after each area George finds a blank book named after a Babylonian month (Nisan,
  Iyyar, Simanu, Duuzu, Abu, Ululu, ... Kislim); writing on the hall door: "If thou seekest the next path, go forth
  with the book." The rooms inside change each time.
- **Middle (10805C-108964):** magic crests on floors; occult books and a diary (Greek/Latin) mention a ritual held
  right when the house was abandoned. The group is Atrahasis, led by Alighieri, who sought eternal life (he was
  about 80). Karol admits he was once an Atrahasis member who quit when the leader began chasing eternal life; he
  came back worried about his old friends. He vows to get George and Alicia out.
- **Later (108D90-1093C0):** a ghost ("????", polite) explains Atrahasis made Alighieri die and revived him to win
  eternal life; the ritual "failed" and they all became ghosts, but "not because of the ritual". Alicia, who hid her
  worry for George, is abducted by a ghost (Karol: surely Alighieri; his talisman didn't work on it). An old ghost
  ("????", じゃ old-man speech -> "my boy", "I'm afraid") says Alighieri DID come back from death, and the ghosts keep
  everyone inside on purpose; gather nine grimoires. George has eight.
- **Glowing books (10B1A4-10B524):** each grimoire glows and reveals a page: eternal life via a journey to death
  and back; the dreadful meaning of it; one who walks in death may come to love death; "one seized by death becomes
  a messenger of death (死のつかい) and will bring death's blessing to all life".
- **Last books (10B5D8-10B734, 10A45C):** the messenger of death would cover the world in death within a year; the
  only cure is sealing him before he wakes, which takes at least nine casters, some of whom may die. Kislim (book 9)
  hides Alighieri's diary: he wanted eternal life at any cost and trusted his friends to seal him if he turned.
- **Climax (1096B4-10A688, 1268AC-126C88):** Karol reasons the seal was incomplete and the ghosts guard the house;
  the 2nd-floor door opens. In the crest room with the coffin, George lays the books on the crest; Karol turns out
  to be the villain (wants "eternal peace", death for all), holding Alicia asleep. Shinfa (the polite ghost, @S)
  breaks his spell by possessing Alicia, George knocks Karol out, and Shinfa guides the re-sealing.
- **Ending (10ABD0):** the ghosts thank George and pass on; Alicia remembers nothing and still won't believe in
  ghosts. Post-credits (11DB88, 11E1DC): jokey fourth-wall skits that unlock Time Attack Mode (Karol hosts; Albino
